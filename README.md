The addons in this repository are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).

Individual addons bundle third-party assets that keep their own licences, and those
terms take precedence over the MIT licence for the files they cover. Tiny Epic
Characters, for example, includes KayKit models and animations under CC BY, with the
licence files packaged alongside them. Check the licence and credit files inside an
addon before redistributing its contents.
